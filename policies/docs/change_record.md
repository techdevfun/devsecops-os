# change_record — a release must have approval, passing gates, evidence

- **Code**: `devsecops.change_record` · **Mechanism**: OPA/Rego over a
  normalised change record (generated by the M4 collector from the PR + gate results) · **Consuming
  gate**: the entire gate suite (results), enforced at the merge step into
  a protected branch / release creation — the final decision layer before release ·
  **Evidence**: `change-record`.

**Which control** (reference mapping): ISO 27001 A.8.32 (change
management) and A.8.9 (configuration management); PCI DSS 4.0 Requirement 6.5 (software
change management); SOC 2 CC8.1 (changes are approved and tested
before deployment); CPS 234 (systematic maintenance of controls).

**Why**: three different framework rows demand the same thing — a change into
an important environment must be traceable to who approved it, what checks ran,
and where the evidence is. The policy consolidates those three demands into a single
machine-readable decision, including a ban on self-approval — a point often overlooked
when relying only on the platform's default settings.

**How to fix a violation**: request a review from someone other than the author; re-run/fix
the gates that have not passed instead of requesting an exception; if an exception is unavoidable,
record a time-limited exception in the record — when it expires the policy blocks again automatically.
